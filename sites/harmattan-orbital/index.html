One pass over Kano

  1. Rise

    The satellite clears the 10° mask on the horizon. The dish is already waiting there.

  2. Track

    The dish turns with it, a few degrees a second at the top of the arc.

  3. Downlink

    S and X band frames come down while it is in view.

  4. Set

    It drops below the mask. Your data is already in your storage.
