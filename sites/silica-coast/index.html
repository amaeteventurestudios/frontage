What leaves our floor

  • Flip-chip bumping

    Solder or copper pillar bumps on your wafers, then flip, reflow and underfill.

  • Wire bond

    Gold and copper wire onto lead frames and laminate substrates, for sensors and power parts.

  • Wafer-level packaging

    Redistribution layers and bumps straight on the wafer, for the smallest finished part.

  • Final test

    Electrical test, burn-in and tape and reel, so parts ship ready for the line.

Lekki Free Zone, Lagos
