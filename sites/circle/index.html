Before you start

Does Circle hold the money?

No. Members pay the receiver as they always have. Circle keeps the record and sends the reminders.

What if someone stops paying?

Your group agrees its rule before round one. Every member sees who has paid each round.

Can two members swap turns?

Yes, when both agree. The order updates for everyone.

Is there interest?

No. Each member puts in and takes out the same total.
