Five voices from one ensemble

  • Ìyá ìlù

    The mother drum. It leads, with the widest bend.

  • Gángan

    Smaller and higher. It answers the lead.

  • Kànnàngó

    The smallest, high and quick.

  • Àtèlé

    Keeps the steady pattern under the talk.

  • Gúdúgúdú

    A bowl drum with a fixed pitch, struck with two sticks.

No voice matches that. Try lead, high or steady.

How the note is made

Strike

A burst of noise for the hand, then the tone of the head.

Squeeze

On the real drum the player squeezes the cords and the head tightens. Here aftertouch or the mod wheel does it.

Body

A resonant filter stands in for the hourglass of carved wood, and colours every note.
